The house needs to know what time it is, whether it is dark, and how to reach you. Build those as small pieces that are *handed* to the code that uses them: a clock the scene can set, a day/night judge, a phone for alerts, and a door watcher that texts you when the front door opens after dark.

Drag the time slider and your `SimClock` follows. Open the door at night and a notification lands on the phone panel; open it at noon and nothing happens.
